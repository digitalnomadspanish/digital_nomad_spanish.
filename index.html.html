DIGITAL NOMAD VISA · SPAIN

Key requirements and important information

An overview of the points to review when considering an application. Your circumstances and the applicable rules should always be checked individually.

Income

The information provided by our team indicates annual income above €36,000. The applicable financial threshold should be confirmed for your application.

Age

Applicants should be over 18 years old.

Education or professional experience

Your academic qualifications or professional experience related to your work may be assessed.

Your own company

If you apply as a business owner, you may be asked to demonstrate that your company has been active for at least three months. This point should be confirmed for your specific case.

Criminal record certificate

A favorable criminal record certificate may be required as part of the application documents.

Travel and visa history

Previous visas and travel history, including travel to Schengen and other countries, may be reviewed as part of the overall file assessment.

Estimated processing time

The estimate shared with us is approximately 20–25 business days. Actual processing times may vary depending on the procedure and individual circumstances.

Professional fees

Fees are provided individually and may depend on the applicant’s country and the details of the case.

These points summarize information shared by our team and are provided for initial guidance only. They are not a definitive or exhaustive statement of legal requirements. Requirements, evidence and processing times can change and must be checked against current rules for each case.
